As part of the FASMA 2026 program, I'm hosting a free experimental watercolor workshop for young artists ages 13 to 20 &mdash; an invitation to explore the medium from a loose, dynamic, and playful angle.

## What to Expect

Over three hours, we'll dig into how water, pigment, and paper actually behave together. Rather than starting with rules, we'll start with experiments: transparencies, layered color, splatters, reserves, unexpected color mixes, and a range of textures &mdash; all aimed at generating effects you can't fully plan for.

That's really the heart of this workshop. In watercolor, accidents and unplanned results aren't mistakes to avoid &mdash; they're some of the most useful material you'll ever get. Instead of chasing perfection, we'll focus on building artistic confidence, the ability to improvise, and the freedom to develop your own visual voice.

I'll be working alongside each participant throughout the session, sharing technical tools and approaches that open up what's possible with contemporary watercolor. Space is limited to 15 participants, so everyone gets real one-on-one time.

## Details

- **Date:** Saturday, August 15, 2026
- **Time:** 11:00 AM &ndash; 2:00 PM
- **Location:** Instituto Allende
- **Who it's for:** Ages 13 to 20
- **Spots available:** 15
- **Cost:** Free
- **Materials:** Included

If you're curious about watercolor and want to try it in an environment where there's no wrong way to make a mark, I'd love to have you there.
